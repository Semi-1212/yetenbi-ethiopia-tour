How to choose your Southern Ethiopia tour

Southern Ethiopia is more than the Omo Valley. The Rift Valley lakes, Arba Minch and the terraced Konso landscape make good shorter trips, and the Omo Valley rewards a longer one. Start with how many days you have.

  • 2 to 4 days: Konso cultural tours or the Arba Minch and Lake Chamo tour.
  • 7 days: the 7-day Omo Valley tour (private) or the 7-day Lalibela and Omo Valley tour (group).
  • 9 to 13 days: the 10-day Omo Valley tour (group) or the 13-day Omo Valley photographic tour.

Looking for Omo Valley tours specifically? See our Omo Valley tours page. Otherwise, send us your dates or message us on WhatsApp and we'll suggest a route.
