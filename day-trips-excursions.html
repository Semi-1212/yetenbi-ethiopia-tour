How to choose your Addis Ababa day trip

Pick by how much time you have and what you want to see. All trips leave from Addis Ababa, and we confirm the route, pickup point and timing with you before you book.

  • Heritage and churches: Debre Libanos and the Blue Nile Gorge, or Adadi Mariam and the Tiya stelae.
  • Lakes and countryside: Bishoftu crater lakes or Wenchi Crater Lake.
  • Hiking: Wechecha, Mogle, Yerer, Zuqualla or Menagesha-Suba Forest.
  • Two days away: Awash, Abijatta Shalla or Hawassa.
  • Short on time? Try our layover tour or Addis Ababa city tours.

Send us your date, group size and hotel location on WhatsApp or through the contact page. We'll confirm the price, transport and guide for your group.
