Detailed Itinerary

Sample Half-Day Addis Ababa Itinerary

A private, customizable half-day route for travelers who want the highlights of Addis Ababa in a shorter visit.

  1. 8:30 AM
    Pickup & route briefing: Meet your guide and driver at your hotel or another agreed pickup point. Confirm your priorities for the visit before setting out.
  2. 9:00 AM
    National Museum of Ethiopia: Explore key exhibits on Ethiopia's history and culture, including the museum's well-known fossil collection, where open and accessible.
  3. 10:30 AM
    Historic city-center stop: Continue to St. George Cathedral or another nearby landmark selected with your guide, subject to opening times and local arrangements.
  4. 11:30 AM
    Merkato or a coffee stop: Choose a guided walk through Merkato market or a traditional Ethiopian coffee ceremony, depending on your interests and remaining time.
  5. 12:30 PM
    Return drop-off: End the tour at your hotel or another agreed drop-off point. Finish time may vary with traffic and the stops selected.

Frequently asked questions

What is included in the half-day Addis Ababa tour?

This half-day itinerary typically includes private transport, guide, hotel or city pickup and drop-off, and bottled water. Entrance fees, meals and personal expenses are not included; confirm final inclusions in your quotation.

Is the half-day city tour private?

Yes. It is designed as a private tour with a dedicated vehicle and guide, ideal for travelers with limited time, including layovers or tight schedules.

Can the route be customized?

Yes. We can adjust the stops, order and pace to suit your interests, pickup point and flight or schedule times.
