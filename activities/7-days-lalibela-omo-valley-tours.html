Lalibela Churches Meets Omo Valley Tribes

This 7-day package is built for travelers who want Ethiopia's two most iconic cultural experiences in one trip: the UNESCO rock-hewn churches of Lalibela and an immersive South Omo Valley tribal safari. You explore living traditions among the Mursi, Hamar, and Karo, then stand inside medieval churches carved from solid rock. Check the Best Time to Visit Ethiopia before locking in your dates.

Domestic flights connect Addis Ababa with Lalibela and Arba Minch so you spend more time on the ground and less on long drives. Whether you want photography time in riverside Karo villages, a chance at a Hamar bull-jumping ceremony, or quiet moments in Lalibela's subterranean chapels, we tailor pacing, lodging, and flight timing to your dates. For camera etiquette and portrait tips with the Mursi, read our Mursi Tribe Photography Guide. Browse more options on our Omo Valley tours page.

Detailed Itinerary

Top 7-Day Lalibela & Omo Valley Itinerary

A day-by-day South Omo Valley and Lalibela tour combining tribal visits in southwestern Ethiopia with the rock-hewn churches of the north. Customizable as request.

  1. Day 1
    Arrive Addis Ababa: Airport welcome, hotel transfer, and optional city orientation. Overnight in Addis Ababa.
  2. Day 2
    Fly Addis to Arba Minch – Konso & Jinka: Morning flight south, drive via Konso UNESCO terraces toward Jinka. Overnight in Jinka.
  3. Day 3
    Mursi Tribe Tour – Jinka to Turmi: Visit Mursi communities near Mago National Park (lip plates and body painting), then continue to Turmi. Overnight in Turmi. Prep with our Mursi Tribe Photography Guide.
  4. Day 4
    Hamar & Karo Cultural Day: Explore Hamar villages and markets; when possible, time for bull-jumping ceremonies. Afternoon visit to Karo riverside settlements for body-art photography. Overnight in Turmi.
  5. Day 5
    Turmi to Arba Minch – Fly Addis: Optional Dassanech or Nyangatom visit if time allows, return to Arba Minch, and fly to Addis Ababa. Overnight in Addis.
  6. Day 6
    Fly to Lalibela – Northern Churches: Fly to Lalibela and explore the northern rock-hewn church cluster, including Bete Medhane Alem and Bete Maryam. Overnight in Lalibela.
  7. Day 7
    Lalibela Southern Cluster – Fly Addis Departure: Visit the southern churches including Bete Giyorgis (St. George), then fly to Addis Ababa for international departure or onward plans.

Frequently asked questions

What is included on the 7 Days Lalibela and Omo Valley Tours?

This itinerary combines Lalibela rock-hewn churches with South Omo Valley tribe visits—typically Mursi, Hamar, and Karo—plus guided transport, listed accommodation, and local cultural experiences.

Is this tour private or group based?

We can arrange it as a private itinerary or adapt it for small groups. Domestic flights, lodging level, and daily pacing can be customized before booking.

Can bull jumping or market days be timed into the itinerary?

When schedules allow, we time South Omo visits around Hamar market days and known bull-jumping ceremonies. Exact ceremony dates are seasonal and confirmed closer to departure.
