Detailed Itinerary

Sample 6-Day Eastern Ethiopia Discovery Itinerary

The route below is indicative and can be tailored. Road travel times, park access and the exact overnight locations are confirmed with the local team before booking.

  1. Day 1
    Addis Ababa to Awash: Depart Addis Ababa by road toward the Afar region, with breaks along the way. Arrive near Awash National Park and settle in for the evening. The exact departure and overnight plan depend on the confirmed transport and accommodation.
  2. Day 2
    Awash National Park: Explore selected park areas with a local guide, taking in savanna and volcanic landscapes. Wildlife viewing, waterfall access and other activities depend on season, park rules, weather and current conditions; sightings are not guaranteed.
  3. Day 3
    Awash to Dire Dawa and Harar: Travel east toward Dire Dawa, with a stop or short orientation visit if time permits, then continue to Harar. Road conditions and the day's timing determine which stops can be included.
  4. Day 4
    Historic Harar: Walk through Harar Jugol, the walled old city, with a local guide. Visit selected lanes, markets, gates and cultural landmarks, allowing time to learn about the city's history and living traditions. Site access and opening times may vary.
  5. Day 5
    Harar and Dire Dawa: Enjoy a relaxed morning for additional Harar visits or a nearby cultural stop agreed with your guide. Continue to Dire Dawa later in the day and explore its historic railway-era quarter as time allows.
  6. Day 6
    Dire Dawa and onward travel: Take a short city visit or free time before your confirmed onward connection. Return transport to Addis Ababa, domestic flights or an extended stay can be discussed and quoted separately according to availability.

This sample itinerary is not a guaranteed schedule. Final route, accommodations, transport, guides, park and site fees, meals and other inclusions are confirmed in the written quote. Wildlife sightings and access to individual locations cannot be guaranteed.
