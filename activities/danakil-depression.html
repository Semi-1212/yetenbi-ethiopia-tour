A guided Danakil Adventure, from 3 to 5 days

Explore the Danakil Depression on a guided 3 to 5 day expedition. This five-day sample shows one possible sequence for Erta Ale, Dallol and Lake Assale; the actual route may change with safety advice, access and weather.

The Danakil is an extremely hot and remote environment. Discuss health considerations, heat tolerance and travel experience when enquiring. Hydration, safety guidance and local access restrictions take priority; activities and overnight stays are never guaranteed.

Transport, guiding, permits, meals and overnight arrangements depend on the confirmed itinerary and are listed in your quote. See our trekking and hiking tours, or send us your dates to plan a route.

Detailed Itinerary

Sample 5-Day Danakil Adventure Itinerary

A suggested five-day expedition sequence. The tour can be shortened or extended; route order, road access, overnight stops and activities depend on current safety advice, permits, heat and local operating conditions.

  1. Day 1
    Travel from Mekele: Meet the local team, review safety procedures and travel toward the confirmed overnight base. Exact timing and overnight location depend on the route arranged.
  2. Day 2
    Explore Dallol and the salt flats: Visit selected hydrothermal areas and salt-flat viewpoints with a local guide. Access depends on permits, weather and current site conditions.
  3. Day 3
    Visit Lake Assale: See Lake Assale and nearby salt-mining areas from approved viewpoints, following local guidance and access restrictions.
  4. Day 4
    Visit Erta Ale volcano: Travel to the Erta Ale area and, only if conditions and safety guidance allow, hike with the local team. Night hiking and overnight arrangements must be confirmed in advance.
  5. Day 5
    Return toward Mekele: Complete the agreed route and transfer toward Mekele. Timing depends on road and weather conditions; confirm onward transport and departure timing in advance.

This is an indicative five-day route within a customizable 3 to 5 day expedition. Erta Ale access, Dallol visits, Lake Assale stops, overnight arrangements and transport must be confirmed with the local operator and may change for safety reasons.
