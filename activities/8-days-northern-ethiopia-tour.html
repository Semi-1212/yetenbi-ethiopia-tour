An eight-day journey through Ethiopia’s historic north

This group route links three of the north’s defining destinations: Lake Tana and Bahir Dar, the castles and churches of Gondar, and Lalibela’s UNESCO-listed rock-hewn churches. The order below makes the travel legs and sightseeing days easy to compare.

Detailed Itinerary

8-Day Northern Ethiopia Historic Route

A suggested group itinerary connecting Bahir Dar and Lake Tana, Gondar, and Lalibela. Transport connections, group dates and opening times must be confirmed before travel.

  1. Day 1
    Day 1 · Arrive in Bahir Dar: Meet the group and settle in after arrival. Depending on arrival time, take a short orientation around the lakeside city or keep the day free to rest. Confirm whether the group package begins with an arrival transfer.
  2. Day 2
    Day 2 · Lake Tana monasteries: Take a boat excursion on Lake Tana to visit selected island or peninsula monasteries. The specific stops depend on boat schedules, site opening and local conditions; return to Bahir Dar later in the day.
  3. Day 3
    Day 3 · Bahir Dar to Gondar: Travel overland from Bahir Dar to Gondar, allowing time for the road journey and scheduled stops. On arrival, visit the Royal Enclosure if timing and opening hours allow; otherwise, the visit moves to the next day.
  4. Day 4
    Day 4 · Gondar’s royal heritage: Continue sightseeing in Gondar, including Fasilides’ Bath and Debre Berhan Selassie Church where open and accessible. Your guide will set the order around opening times and local arrangements.
  5. Day 5
    Day 5 · Gondar to Lalibela: Transfer from Gondar to Lalibela using the transport arranged for the group. This is a significant travel leg, so the arrival time and any sightseeing depend on the confirmed connection; plan the day primarily for travel.
  6. Day 6
    Lalibela’s northern church cluster: Explore the northern group of rock-hewn churches with a local guide. Visit sequence and time at each church depend on access, service schedules and the group’s pace.
  7. Day 7
    Lalibela’s southern cluster & Biete Ghiorgis: Continue guided visits to the southern church group, including Biete Ghiorgis where accessible. The local guide will confirm the day’s route and allow time for breaks.
  8. Day 8
    Departure from Lalibela: Transfer for your onward journey. Confirm flight or ground transport arrangements, departure time and any included transfer in your booking details.

This is a suggested route, not a promise of specific transport connections or site access. The group departure schedule, flights or overland transfers, accommodation, meals, entrance fees and guide services should be confirmed in the written itinerary and quote.
