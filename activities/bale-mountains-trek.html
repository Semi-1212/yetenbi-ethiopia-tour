A flexible Bale Mountains trek, from 2 to 6 days

Trek Bale Mountains National Park with a local guide. Choose a 2 to 6 day route through Afro-alpine moorland, the Sanetti Plateau and Harenna Forest. The five-day sample below shows one possible way to combine day walks and overnight stops.

We tailor walking distance, elevation gain and accommodation to your dates, fitness and season. High altitude can affect everyone differently, so share your trekking experience and any relevant health considerations when you enquire. Wildlife sightings and trail access vary with conditions.

Transport, guiding, park fees, meals and accommodation depend on the route and are confirmed in your quote. See our trekking and hiking tours, or send us your dates to plan a route.

Detailed Itinerary

Sample 5-Day Bale Mountains Trek Itinerary

A suggested five-day route through selected areas of Bale Mountains National Park. The route can be shortened or extended; trail choice, overnight stops and walking times depend on the agreed trip length and current conditions.

  1. Day 1
    Arrive in Robe or Dinsho: Meet your local team in Robe or Dinsho and review the route, equipment and park arrangements. The overnight location depends on arrival timing and the confirmed transport plan.
  2. Day 2
    Enter the park and explore Dinsho: Complete park formalities and take a guided walk near Dinsho, looking for endemic wildlife such as the mountain nyala where conditions permit. Your guide adjusts the walking pace to the group.
  3. Day 3
    Explore the Sanetti Plateau: Travel to the Sanetti Plateau for a guided walk across its high-altitude moorland. The route and time outdoors depend on weather, road access and how the group is responding to the altitude.
  4. Day 4
    Walk toward Harenna Forest: Descend toward Harenna Forest and explore a selected trail with your guide. Forest access, wildlife viewing and the overnight location depend on local conditions and the confirmed itinerary.
  5. Day 5
    Return toward Robe or Dinsho: Finish the planned trek and transfer toward Robe or Dinsho. Confirm onward transport and departure timing in advance, allowing for road and weather variations.

This is an indicative five-day route within a customizable 2 to 6 day trek. Specific trails, wildlife sightings, accommodation and logistics are not guaranteed and must be confirmed with the local team.
