Visit Ethiopia’s Most Significant Islamic Heritage Site

Al Nejashi Mosque in the village of Negash is revered as one of the earliest places of Islam in Africa. It is associated with the First Hijra, when Companions of the Prophet sought refuge in Ethiopia under the just king known as Al Nejashi. This 2-day pilgrimage lets you visit the site, learn its story and spend time in reflection.

The itinerary is paced around prayer times and can be shaped to your group's needs. It can also be extended with Mekelle, Wukro and other heritage sites in Tigray, or combined with our longer Islamic heritage tours. Browse all Islamic pilgrimage tours for more options.

Detailed Itinerary

2-Day Al Nejashi Mosque Pilgrimage Itinerary

A private two-day pilgrimage from Addis Ababa to Negash, with local guidance and flexible timing.

  1. Day 1
    Addis Ababa to Mekelle: Welcome to Ethiopia! On arrival you will be met at Bole International Airport, then take the flight to Mekelle. On arrival in Mekelle, enjoy a halal breakfast and a city tour, visiting Hawelti and the Martyrs’ Memorial Museum, with a walk and drive through the streets of Mekelle.

    You will then pray Zuhr and Asr at the Grand Anwar Mosque and have a halal lunch at a halal restaurant.

    In the afternoon, drive to Wukro and Al Nejashi Mosque, the first mosque in Africa, dating from the 7th century. There you will visit the graves of 15 Sahabas (Companions of the Prophet Muhammad, PBUH), pray, recite Ayahs of the Quran and enjoy sightseeing.

    Overnight stay in Mekelle.

  2. Day 2
    Mekelle to Addis Ababa & Departure: After breakfast, transfer to the airport for your flight to Addis Ababa, where you will have a full-day city tour. You will see two of the largest mosques in Addis Ababa, the Nur (Beni) Mosque and the Anwar Mosque.

    Lunch will be at one of the Muslim restaurants in Addis Ababa, followed by a visit to the National Museum of Ethiopia and some time for shopping.

    In the evening, enjoy a halal farewell dinner, then transfer to the airport for your flight home. End of tour.

Frequently asked questions

What is included on the 2-day Al Nejashi pilgrimage?

The tour typically includes private ground transport, an English-speaking guide, accommodation, listed entrance fees and meals as specified in your quotation.

Is Al Nejashi Mosque accessible to visitors?

Yes. Visitors are welcome, with respectful dress and etiquette. Your guide will advise on timing and local customs on the day.

Can the pilgrimage be extended?

Yes. We can add Mekelle, Wukro, Addis Ababa or other Islamic heritage sites, such as in our 3-day Al Nejashi and Addis Ababa tour.
