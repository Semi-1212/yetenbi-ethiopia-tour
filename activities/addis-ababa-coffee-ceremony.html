Ethiopian Coffee Culture

Ethiopian Coffee Ceremony in Addis Ababa

Discover Ethiopia’s coffee story on a private experience that moves from the traditional jebena ceremony to specialty roasting and guided tasting, with hotel or airport pickup.

4–5 hours Addis Ababa Private pickup From $55
View Detailed Itinerary Plan by WhatsApp

A private journey into Ethiopian coffee

Your experience begins with pickup from your hotel, Bole International Airport or another agreed location. Meet your guide and explore coffee’s importance in Ethiopian culture, hospitality and everyday community life. The route and timing can be adjusted to your plans.

Detailed Itinerary

  1. 01

    Pickup & Introduction to Ethiopian Coffee

    Begin with private pickup from your hotel, Bole International Airport or another agreed location in Addis Ababa. Meet your guide for an introduction to Ethiopia’s relationship with coffee and its importance in culture, hospitality and community life.

    Experience: Ethiopian coffee history, culture and origins

  2. 02

    Traditional Ethiopian Coffee Ceremony

    Watch green coffee beans being freshly roasted before they are ground and brewed in a traditional clay coffee pot called a jebena. Learn about the ceremony’s cultural importance and enjoy freshly prepared coffee in a traditional setting.

    Experience: Roasting, grinding, jebena brewing and traditional coffee service

  3. 03

    Specialty Coffee & Roasting Experience

    Continue to a selected specialty coffee venue, roaster or coffee facility in Addis Ababa. Learn how coffee moves from origin to cup, including processing, grading, roasting and preparation. Depending on the venue and daily operations, you may also observe roasting, sorting or packaging.

    Experience: Ethiopian specialty coffee, roasting and bean-to-cup knowledge

  4. 04

    Coffee Tasting & Cupping

    Finish with a guided coffee tasting or professional-style cupping, subject to availability. Compare coffees from regions such as Yirgacheffe, Sidama, Guji and Harar, depending on what is available that day. Learn how coffee professionals identify aroma, acidity, body, sweetness and other flavor characteristics.

    Experience: Coffee tasting, origin comparison and cupping where available

  5. 05

    Return Transfer

    At the end of the 4–5-hour experience, your driver will transfer you back to your hotel, Bole International Airport or another agreed drop-off point. For transit travelers, the schedule can be adjusted according to your onward flight time.

    Experience: Private return transfer tailored to your schedule

Package Includes

  • Private pickup and return transfer in Addis Ababa
  • Guide-led introduction to Ethiopian coffee culture
  • Traditional coffee ceremony experience with freshly prepared coffee
  • Visit to a selected specialty coffee venue or roaster
  • Guided coffee tasting or cupping, when available

Exact inclusions and any venue charges will be confirmed with your booking.

Package Excludes

  • Flights to or from Addis Ababa
  • Meals and drinks beyond coffee included in the ceremony or tasting
  • Personal purchases, coffee beans and souvenirs
  • Tips and gratuities
  • Any optional expenses not listed in your confirmed booking

This is a suggested itinerary. Specialty venues, roasting observations and coffee varieties depend on availability and daily operations. Confirm pickup, timing and inclusions when booking.
