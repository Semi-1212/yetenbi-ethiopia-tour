A flexible Simien Mountains trek, from 3 to 12 days

Trek the highlands of the Simien Mountains with a local guide. Shorter 3 to 5 day trips focus on selected trails and viewpoints. Longer trips of up to 12 days allow a slower pace and more trekking days. The seven-day route below shows how a typical trek progresses.

We adjust the route to your dates, fitness and the season, and you confirm the final plan with us before you book. The Simien Mountains are high, so tell us about your trekking experience and health when you enquire. A summit attempt is optional.

Meals, camping equipment and camp crew are not included, and your quote lists exactly what is covered. See our trekking and hiking tours, or send us your dates to plan a route.

Detailed Itinerary

Sample 7-Day Simien Mountains Trek Itinerary

A suggested seven-day trekking route through selected areas of the Simien Mountains. The route can be shortened or extended; trail choice, camp locations and walking times depend on the agreed trip length and current conditions.

  1. Day 1
    Arrive in Gondar or Debark: Meet the local team and review the proposed route, equipment and park arrangements. The overnight location depends on arrival timing and the confirmed transport plan.
  2. Day 2
    Travel to the park and begin trekking: Transfer to the agreed trailhead and start with a measured walk to allow time to adjust to the altitude. The guide sets the pace and destination according to conditions.
  3. Day 3
    Trek toward the Geech area: Continue along the chosen highland trail through open scenery and viewpoints. Stop as needed for rest, hydration and gradual acclimatization before reaching the planned camp.
  4. Day 4
    Explore the Geech plateau: Take a guided day walk to nearby viewpoints and return to camp. The exact route and walking time are adjusted for weather, altitude response and group ability.
  5. Day 5
    Continue toward the Chenek area: Follow the selected trail toward Chenek, taking time for landscape stops and breaks. The guide confirms the camp location and route based on current park conditions.
  6. Day 6
    Guided walk near camp: Use this day for a local walk, wildlife observation and scenery around the camp area. Activities are subject to safe access, visibility, weather and the group's fitness.
  7. Day 7
    Exit the trekking area: Finish the trek and transfer toward Debark or Gondar for onward travel. The departure connection should be arranged with enough time for road and weather variations.

This is an indicative route only. The 3 to 12 day trek is customized; specific trails, camps, walking hours, wildlife sightings, summit options and logistics are not guaranteed and must be confirmed with the local team.
