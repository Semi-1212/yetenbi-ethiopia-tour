Historic North Circuit

Northern Ethiopia Tours: Lalibela, Gondar, Axum & Tigray

Walk Lalibela’s rock-hewn churches, cruise Lake Tana to island monasteries, explore royal Gondar and Axum’s stelae, or climb to Tigray’s cliff churches. Private or group itineraries from 3 to 14 days.

  • Lalibela & rock churches
  • Lake Tana & Gondar
  • Tigray & Gheralta
  • Axum & Simien trekking
Browse Northern Tours Plan via WhatsApp

How to choose your Northern Ethiopia tour

Start with how many days you have. Every tour starts in Addis Ababa, and prices are per person, with a "From" price where the cost depends on group size.

  • 3 to 4 days: Lalibela rock-hewn churches, or Lake Tana, royal Gondar and Gorgora.
  • 5 days in Tigray: Tigray churches or the Mekelle and Gheralta heritage tour.
  • 6 to 8 days: the 6-day historic route (group), the 7-day Gorgora and historic north (private), or the 8-day northern tour (group).
  • 11 to 14 days: the historic route with Simien trekking or the full 14-day northern circuit with Axum and Tigray.

Not sure which fits? Send us your dates, group size and budget on WhatsApp or through the contact page, and we'll suggest a route.
