How to choose your Eastern Ethiopia tour

Eastern Ethiopia combines Awash National Park, Harar's UNESCO-listed old city, Dire Dawa, Babile and the Danakil Depression with Dallol. Start with how many days you have.

  • 1 to 2 days: a day trip to Awash National Park, or the 2-day Awash tour (group).
  • 3 days: Harar and Dire Dawa for walled-city culture and markets.
  • 5 to 6 days: the 5-day Awash, Harar, Dire Dawa and Babile tour (group), the 5-day Danakil Depression and Dallol expedition, or the 6-day Eastern Ethiopia discovery tour (private).
  • 12 days: the 12-day Eastern Ethiopia and Danakil tour (private), which joins all three regions.
