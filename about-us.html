About Yetenbi Ethiopia Tours: Your Local Tour Operator in Addis Ababa

We are a team of local guides and trip planners based in Addis Ababa. We run cultural, coffee, historical and hiking tours across Ethiopia, plus layover tours and private custom trips built around you.

Local Ethiopia Tours With Yetenbi

Yetenbi Ethiopia Tours was created to help travelers experience Ethiopia beyond the usual tourist routes. As a licensed and certified tour operator, we believe the best journeys are built around meaningful encounters, local knowledge, and the freedom to discover a place at your own pace.

The name Yetenbi, meaning “Welcome” in Gurage, reflects the spirit of hospitality at the heart of our work. We want every traveler to feel welcomed, respected, and connected to the people and places they visit. Our goal is to become Ethiopia’s #1 trusted and reliable tour operator, known for quality service, authentic experiences, and responsible tourism.

Based in Addis Ababa, we organize journeys throughout Ethiopia, from historic cities and ancient landmarks to remote landscapes and vibrant communities. Our team works with local guides and trusted partners to create thoughtful travel experiences that are comfortable, flexible, and respectful of the people and places we visit.
